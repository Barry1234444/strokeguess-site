Privacy Policy — StrokeGuess

StrokeGuess · Version 1.0.0 · Effective November 20, 2026

1. What we collect

We do not collect personal information from the app.

StrokeGuess has no cloud and no server of ours. The app does not require an account or login, and does not collect your real name, email, phone number, location, photos, contacts, device identifier, advertising identifier, or usage analytics. We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, tracking, or push-notification SDKs.

Display names and drawings stay on your local Wi-Fi and are used only to play the game in your room. They are never uploaded to us. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply.

2. How the app works

StrokeGuess is a party drawing-and-guessing game for people in the same room.

  • An Apple TV app hosts the game on your local Wi-Fi.
  • Players join from a phone browser on the same Wi-Fi and draw on the phone. The phone loads a page from the Apple TV on your local network, not from a website we host.
  • The drawing appears on the TV, and everyone else shouts their guess in the room.
  • Players take turns passing the drawing role. The game is designed for about 2–8 people.

There is no account, no online public feed, no chat, no cloud sync, and no play with people outside your local network.

3. What stays in your room and on your devices

During a game, other people in the same room can see display names, drawings, and scores on the TV. This information is held in memory on the Apple TV and travels only between the Apple TV and the phones on your Wi-Fi. It is never sent to us, is not published online, and is cleared when you quit the app or end the match.

A phone browser may store the display name you chose on that phone. That preference stays on that phone, is never uploaded or synced to us, and is deleted when you clear the browser data.

4. Purchases

StrokeGuess is sold on the App Store. Payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card information, and buying the app does not require an account with us. If optional in-app content is offered, those purchases are also processed by Apple.

5. Permissions and third-party services

The Apple TV app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location. Players draw by touching the phone screen in the browser; the app does not record the room or listen to guesses.

The Apple TV app requests Local Network permission so it can host a small game room on your Wi-Fi. Phones on the same network open that local page to join and draw. The app may advertise the room with Bonjour on your local network only. Gameplay data does not leave your Wi-Fi.

We do not use third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and we do not send gameplay data to any server of ours. If a future version adds a third-party service, a permission, advertising, or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

6. Children

StrokeGuess is a family-friendly party game. Drawings and display names are created by the players in the room and are shown only to the people playing together; they are not posted online. Children should play with a parent or guardian nearby, as with any TV party game.

7. Changes to this policy and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same public URL and the date above will be updated.
